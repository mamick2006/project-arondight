v2.0.0
Not save compatible with previous versions of the mod. Can be added to existing saves.

- Updated for Starsector 0.98.5a.
- Completely redesigned UI.
- The Manage button no longer overrides your own keybinding for Y.
- Added a setting for the Manage shortcut key.
- Fixed Broceliande Station, the Dead Relay and the Deep Anchorage showing their earlier dialog again after you finished their mission.
- Fixed Broceliande Station, the Dead Relay and the Deep Anchorage showing no description on hover.
- Added a one-time note on where the quest starts, and the salvor in the bar now mentions the mech.
- Fixed the proving course being offered again before unpausing, which gave extra Arondights.
- Fixed the proving course walls stopping the mech early and catching it on door corners.
- The proving course now says to reach the exit gate.
- Added a setting to skip the proving course.
- Split respec into Reset Form and Reset Weave.
- Added an Undo button for weaves and form changes.
- Afterimage now blinks along the mech's movement.
- Static Bleed does more damage.
- Spotter Lattice's target now takes 30% more damage instead of 50%.
- Durendal, Hauteclere and Joyeuse have more hull and dissipate flux faster. Durendal and Joyeuse also have more armor.
- Every form of the ship costs 5 less supplies per month, recovery cost and deployment points.
- Each weave now adds 1 to supplies per month, recovery cost and deployment points instead of 2.
- Added three nodes to the System branch of the weave: Surge Grid, Downtime Routine and Purge Valves.
- You can now invest up to 5 plus your level in story points, up from your level.
- Recovering the mech now gives 3 story points.
- Updated the weave icons.
- In Survival Mode, the quarantine watch at Broceliande Station is a much smaller fleet.
- Reinforced Weave, Reactor Bloom, Drive Resonance, Neural Mesh, Expanded Lattice, Zero-Point Idle and Kinetic Buffer are stronger.
- Gae Bolg's weapons now lock for 3 seconds instead of 5.
- Destroying a fighter no longer counts for Harvest Reflex or for removing Gae Bolg's lock.
- Fixed Joyeuse's ward not returning damage to the attacker.
- Fixed Loom Salvage giving no extra salvage after battle.
- Hopefully fixed HUD mods listing the mech's decorative parts as weapons.
- Fixed a crash when some modded weapons fired from the arm or shoulder mounts.
- Added an ArondightRecallFrame console command that brings back a lost mech.
- Fixed the Dead Relay's escort losing all its combat readiness when the relay is close to a star.
- Redid all animations and sprites to be dynamic and detailed, and more like mechs, with many different moving components
